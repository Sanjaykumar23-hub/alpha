Typewriting — English

Lower & Higher Grade
▼

Our English Typewriting programme is designed to prepare students for the Government Technical Examinations (GTE) conducted by DOTE, Tamil Nadu. Training is done on both manual and electronic typewriters with a strong focus on speed and zero-error accuracy.

Junior Grade (Lower)

Covers basic touch-typing, finger positioning, posture, and layout familiarity. Target speed: 30 WPM.

Senior Grade (Higher)

Intensive training for high-speed text production, official letter formats, and statement typing. Target speed: 45+ WPM.

Computer on Office Automation (COA)

DOTE Certified
▼

COA is the most critical certification for Tamil Nadu state government job eligibility. This course combines 40 hours of theory and 60 hours of mandatory practical lab work, covering all tools needed for modern government and corporate office environments.

Syllabus Coverage

Operating Systems (Windows & Linux), MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, MS Access, Internet & Email, State Portal Navigation.

Exam Structure

Theory paper (40 marks) + Practical examination (60 marks). Certificate issued directly by DOTE, Tamil Nadu upon passing.

  • 🎯Target Roles
    DEO, Office Assistant, Junior Clerk in state departments
  • 📋Eligibility
    SSLC pass + Junior Typewriting (English or Tamil)
  • 🕐Batch Timings
    6:30–10:00 AM / 3:00–8:30 PM
  • 📅Days
    Monday to Saturday
